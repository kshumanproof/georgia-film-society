Logo usage

Keep the mark clear, restrained, and intact.

Use approved files

Use the official logo files provided on this page. Do not recreate the logo manually.

Preserve spacing

Give the logo enough room to breathe. Avoid crowding the mark with text, graphics, or partner logos.

Do not distort

Do not stretch, skew, crop, rotate, recolor, add effects, or alter the proportions of the mark.

Maintain contrast

Use the logo on backgrounds that provide clear contrast and preserve legibility.

File notes
