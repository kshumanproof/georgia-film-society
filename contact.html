Frequently asked questions

A few practical answers as the institution develops.

What is Georgia Film Society? +

Georgia Film Society is a nonprofit cinematic cultural institution for Georgia and the Southeast, built around storytelling, cinema culture, filmmaker support, membership, patronage, partnerships, and future programming.

Is GFS a nonprofit? +

Georgia Film Society is a registered 501(c)3 nonprofit institution. Its public programming, membership, donor support, and partnerships are being built with long-term cultural benefit in mind.

What is Cardenne Film Festival? +

Cardenne Film Festival is the flagship festival of Georgia Film Society. It is designed as a prestige discovery platform built around merit, curation, hospitality, and serious cinematic work.

Is Cardenne the same as GFS? +

No. Georgia Film Society is the larger institution. Cardenne is the flagship festival brand inside the GFS ecosystem.

How do I become a member? +

Visit the Membership page to choose an annual membership tier. Membership supports the institution while creating access to future screenings, conversations, member events, early announcements, and cultural programming.

How do I donate? +

Visit the Support page to make a contribution or explore specific giving areas such as student access, programming, founding support, and institutional development.

Can my company sponsor or partner with GFS? +

Yes. GFS is developing partnership pathways for sponsors, civic partners, hospitality partners, universities, technology companies, cultural organizations, and aligned institutions. Visit the Partner page or email hello@georgiafilmsociety.com.

Are programs active now? +

GFS is currently building its institutional foundation. Future programming may include screenings, salons, filmmaker conversations, student access, summits, member events, and Cardenne-related updates.

How can students or volunteers get involved? +

Students, educators, interns, and volunteers can email hello@georgiafilmsociety.com with the subject line “Student or Volunteer Interest.”

Who should press contact? +

Press inquiries can be sent to hello@georgiafilmsociety.com with the subject line “Press Inquiry.”
