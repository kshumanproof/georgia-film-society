Volunteer

Help shape the atmosphere around cinema.

Georgia Film Society relies on thoughtful, reliable volunteers to support screenings, salons, member gatherings, hospitality, student access, The Aster-related programs, and cultural events.

Apply to Volunteer Student Access

Volunteer philosophy

Volunteers help define how the institution feels in the room.

Georgia Film Society volunteers are part of the atmosphere around the work. They help create the welcome, calm, rhythm, and operational seriousness that filmmakers, members, students, patrons, and audiences feel when they gather around cinema.

The work is practical, but the standard is cultural: care, preparation, hospitality, discretion, and respect for the room.

Who we are looking for

Reliable, thoughtful people who understand the value of atmosphere.

Volunteers help make the room feel considered, organized, and welcoming without drawing attention away from the work.

Calm under pressure

Events move quickly. Volunteers help make the room feel easier, not louder.

Guest-minded

Hospitality, clear communication, and respect for members, artists, partners, and audiences matter.

Dependable

Showing up, following instructions, and communicating clearly help carry the standard of the institution.

Aligned

Volunteers understand that GFS is shaped by care, preparation, discretion, and respect for the work.

Volunteer process

A thoughtful process for a serious volunteer team.

The application helps Georgia Film Society understand availability, alignment, and role fit before matching volunteers with the right opportunities.

01

Apply to volunteer

02

Review and fit

03

Orientation
