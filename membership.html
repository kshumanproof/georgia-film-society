Membership

Belong to the cultural life being built around cinema.

Membership is the doorway into Georgia Film Society. Members help sustain the institution while receiving access to screenings, conversations, member events, early announcements, and the year-round cinema culture being built across Georgia and the Southeast.

Membership philosophy

This is not a discount program. Membership is a way to participate in the formation of a cinematic cultural institution.

Founding Circle

For founding patrons, families, executives, and institutional supporters.

Founding Circle is a relationship-based patron circle for major support, recognition, private conversations, and future underwriting opportunities. It is not a standard checkout product.
