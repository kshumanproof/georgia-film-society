Cardennes Film Festival

The flagship festival of Georgia Film Society.

Cardennes Film Festival is a prestige discovery platform built around merit, curation, hospitality, and serious cinematic work.

Visit Cardennes Festival Updates

The crown jewel, not the whole crown.

Georgia Film Society is the institution. Cardennes is its flagship festival expression.

Festival positioning

Cardennes is designed as a boutique prestige festival where selection means something: a festival of taste, atmosphere, hospitality, and cinematic excellence.

Festival atmosphere

Cinema, hospitality, discovery, and serious creative work.

Curation

A festival built around serious selection.

Cardennes should feel selective, intentional, and filmmaker-respecting. The goal is not volume. The goal is discovery with credibility.

Filmmakers

Filmmaker-first, story-first, merit-first.

The festival should treat selected artists as serious professionals, not content suppliers.

Relationship to GFS

Cardennes is the flagship. Georgia Film Society is the larger cultural engine.

The Georgia Film Society site should make visitors aware of Cardennes, explain its role, and route festival-specific traffic to the festival’s own home.

Submissions, tickets, passes, schedule, festival press, festival sponsors, and festival news should live with Cardennes. GFS remains the institution that makes the flagship possible.

Hospitality

Atmosphere is part of the festival’s value.

Cardennes should feel curated before the first screening begins. Hospitality, venue experience, arrival, conversation, and member/patron presence all contribute to the credibility of the festival.

The festival should be sophisticated without becoming fake luxury, intimate without becoming small, and ambitious without becoming noisy.

Host ecosystem

Built for an elevated destination experience.

Alpharetta and Avalon remain a strong target host ecosystem because of their walkability, hospitality infrastructure, modern environment, and proximity to metro Atlanta.

Public language should remain strategically flexible. Cardennes is designed as a prestige festival brand with the ability to serve the right host environment as partnerships develop.
