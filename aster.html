The Aster Film Festival

A selective festival for films that deserve serious attention.

The Aster is the flagship festival of Georgia Film Society, recognizing exceptional work within a refined setting for filmmakers, audiences, patrons, and industry guests.

Visit The Aster Join Updates

Festival positioning

The Aster is designed as a selective, filmmaker-respecting festival where each invitation carries weight and every detail supports the work on screen.

Festival atmosphere

Intimate, distinctive, and worthy of meaningful attention.

Selection

Selection should carry weight.

The Aster is shaped around intentional programming, not volume. Discovery should feel credible, and selected work should receive the attention it deserves.

Filmmakers

The work comes first.

Selected filmmakers should feel that the festival understands the discipline, sacrifice, and ambition behind serious cinematic work.

Relationship to GFS

The Aster is the flagship festival. Georgia Film Society is the institution behind it.

The Festival

The Aster carries the public festival experience.

Submissions, tickets, passes, schedules, festival press, sponsor details, and official festival updates belong with The Aster.

The Institution

GFS sustains the cultural foundation around it.

Georgia Film Society supports the broader ecosystem: membership, patronage, education, partnerships, year-round programs, and long-term cultural infrastructure.

Hospitality

The experience begins before the lights go down.

The Aster is shaped by how people arrive, gather, converse, and feel welcomed. The festival experience should honor the work while making filmmakers, guests, patrons, and audiences feel considered.

The goal is refinement without distance, intimacy without smallness, and ambition without noise.

Host ecosystem

A festival should feel connected to its setting.

The Aster is developed around walkability, atmosphere, hospitality, and a host environment that can support screenings, gatherings, dining, conversation, and discovery.

The setting should feel polished, accessible, and alive beyond the theater doors.

Festival bridge

Visit The Aster for festival details.

Submissions, passes, schedules, announcements, press materials, and festival-specific updates are housed with The Aster.
