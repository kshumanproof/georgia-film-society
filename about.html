About the institution

A home for cinema culture in Georgia and the Southeast.

Georgia Film Society exists to treat cinema as more than entertainment. It is building a cultural home for the people who make, support, study, preserve, and gather around film.

Mission

To cultivate cinema culture, support serious filmmakers, and build a year-round ecosystem around cinematic excellence.

Vision

To help Georgia and the Southeast become defining contributors to the future of cinematic storytelling.

Values

Taste, curation, hospitality, merit, filmmaker respect, access, restraint, and long-term cultural legitimacy.

Why Georgia

Georgia has become one of the most important production centers in modern entertainment. But production infrastructure alone does not create cultural leadership.

Great artistic regions are strengthened through institutions, community, hospitality, creative ambition, and places that still believe the work matters.

Georgia Film Society is being built to help create that kind of cultural infrastructure.

Founder-level philosophy

Human stories still matter. Georgia Film Society is being built with the long view in mind: a cultural institution rooted in storytelling, artistic ambition, hospitality, creative discovery, and the belief that great work still deserves serious support.
