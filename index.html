What we are building

Georgia Film Society exists to elevate cinema as culture. Through curated events, filmmaker-centered programming, membership experiences, educational initiatives, and future-facing conversations, GFS is building an institution designed to serve storytellers and audiences long after any single festival weekend ends.

Three pillars

Cinema Culture

Curated screenings, salons, conversations, and gatherings that treat cinema as a serious cultural form.

Filmmaker Support

A filmmaker-first institution built around merit, hospitality, craft, and respect for the work.

Future Storytelling

Thoughtful programming around the future of cinema, technology, ethics, creativity, and audience culture.
