Why Georgia. Why now.

Georgia has become a production center. Now it deserves a deeper cultural home.

Production can bring work to a region. Institutions help give that work lasting cultural weight.

Georgia and the South are entering a defining creative era. The state already supports filmmakers, writers, producers, craftspeople, entrepreneurs, technologists, and creative leaders whose influence extends beyond tax incentives or production logistics.

Georgia Film Society exists for what comes next: curated screenings, salons, education, membership, patronage, filmmaker support, cultural conversation, and a flagship festival worthy of serious attention.
