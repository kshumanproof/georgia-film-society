Students & Education

Student access to serious cinema culture.

Georgia Film Society helps emerging filmmakers, students, interns, and educators enter meaningful creative rooms through reduced membership, volunteer pathways, screenings, salons, mentorship, and education partnerships.

Request Student Access Student Volunteer

Why student access matters

The next generation should have a real seat in the room.

Georgia Film Society is building student access into the institution from the beginning, so emerging filmmakers, writers, artists, and storytellers can participate in serious cinema culture with dignity and purpose.

Through reduced membership, volunteer pathways, education partnerships, screenings, salons, and future mentorship opportunities, students can encounter film as art, craft, community, and creative possibility.

Student Access Membership

How approval works

Request access

Receive a private link

Approved students receive next steps and a private reduced membership checkout link.
