Press Inquiry Approved Language

Media note

Georgia Film Society should be described as a nonprofit cinematic cultural institution for Georgia and the Southeast — not a film club, startup nonprofit, or festival-only organization.

Approved language

Use this language when describing the institution.

Short description

Georgia Film Society is a nonprofit cinematic cultural institution for Georgia and the Southeast.

Standard description

Georgia Film Society is a nonprofit cinematic cultural institution dedicated to storytelling, artistic excellence, filmmaker support, cultural conversation, education, membership, patronage, and the future of film in Georgia and the Southeast.

Extended description

Georgia Film Society is being built as a year-round cultural institution for cinema, filmmakers, patrons, students, partners, and audiences across Georgia and the Southeast. Its long-term ecosystem may include screenings, salons, education initiatives, filmmaker-centered programming, memberships, patronage, partnerships, future-of-storytelling conversations, and Cardennes Film Festival as its flagship festival brand.

Cardennes language

The flagship festival of Georgia Film Society.

Cardennes Film Festival is the flagship festival of Georgia Film Society: a prestige discovery platform built around merit, curation, hospitality, and serious cinematic work.

Cardennes should be described as part of the larger Georgia Film Society ecosystem, not as a replacement for the institution.

Language to avoid

Keep the public framing institution-first.

Not a club

Avoid describing GFS as a local film club or casual community group.

Not festival-only

Cardennes is the flagship festival, but GFS is the larger institution.

Not startup language

Avoid hype-first, gimmicky, or startup-style framing.

Not generic arts copy

Avoid vague “support the arts” language as the main institutional description.
