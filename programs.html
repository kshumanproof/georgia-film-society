Programs

A year-round ecosystem for cinema culture.

Georgia Film Society brings people together through curated screenings, member salons, filmmaker conversations, student access, writer-centered programs, The Aster, and conversations around the evolving language of cinema.

Built with restraint

Programs with purpose, not noise.

Georgia Film Society is being built around rooms worth gathering in: screenings, salons, filmmaker conversations, student access, writer-focused programs, The Aster, and serious dialogue about the future of cinema.

The Society’s programs are shaped around cultural purpose, filmmaker respect, hospitality, education, and meaningful gathering — not speed, spectacle, or empty scale.

The work extends beyond a single event.

Each program area is designed to create access, deepen conversation, and give filmmakers, members, students, patrons, and guests a reason to return.

Merit. Curation. Hospitality.

The Aster is Georgia Film Society’s flagship festival: selective, discovery-minded, filmmaker-respecting, and built around a carefully shaped guest experience.

It gives the Society a festival platform while the broader institution continues year-round through screenings, salons, education, writer programs, partnerships, and public conversation.
