Flagship Festival

Cardenne Film Festival

Part of the larger ecosystem

The flagship festival of Georgia Film Society.

Cardenne is being developed as a prestige discovery festival centered on merit, curation, hospitality, and serious cinematic work.

The festival is one expression of the larger institution: a year-round home for screenings, salons, education, patronage, partnerships, and future-facing conversations around cinema.
