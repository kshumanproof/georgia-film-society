Partnership philosophy

Partnership with Georgia Film Society should feel like cultural alignment, not logo placement. The right partners help build an institution around storytelling, hospitality, education, civic identity, and creative leadership.

Areas of alignment

Partnerships can shape the atmosphere, access, and cultural reach of the institution.

Cultural Alignment

Cinema, conversation, and civic life.

Partners can help support screenings, salons, filmmaker conversations, cultural gatherings, and the public life of cinema in Georgia.

Hospitality

A more intentional experience.

Hospitality partners can help shape the atmosphere surrounding screenings, festivals, dinners, salons, visiting filmmakers, and member gatherings.

Future Storytelling

Creativity, technology, and what comes next.

Technology, education, and innovation partners can help create thoughtful space for conversations about cinema, AI, authorship, ethics, and creative culture.

05

Screening Partner

06

Education Partner

Universities, schools, and education partners supporting access, mentorship, internships, and learning.

07

Student Access Partner

08

Member Events Partner

Alignment with private salons, member gatherings, dinners, Q&As, and cultural experiences.

Why partner

GFS is being built for cultural gravity, not short-term noise.

Prestige alignment

Associate with a restrained, cinematic, institution-first cultural brand.

Audience quality

Reach filmmakers, patrons, members, civic leaders, students, industry guests, and culturally engaged audiences.

Regional relevance

Support Georgia and the Southeast as a serious creative and cultural ecosystem.

Long-term visibility

Partner with an institution designed for membership, programming, education, festivals, and future growth.

Hospitality value

Create memorable experiences around screenings, salons, visiting filmmakers, civic gatherings, and festival guests.

Future-facing identity

Participate in conversations about storytelling, technology, ethics, innovation, and creative culture.

Cardennes Film Festival

A flagship sponsorship platform inside a larger institution.

Cardennes creates a prestige festival environment for discovery, hospitality, filmmakers, audiences, sponsors, press, and cultural visibility.

Explore Cardennes

Cardennes is one partnership platform, not the only one.

Partners may align with the festival, year-round screenings, future summits, student access, member events, hospitality, education, civic programming, or broader institutional development.

Partnership process

Built through conversation, not checkout.

1. Start the conversation

Share your organization, interest area, and the kind of alignment you want to explore.

2. Review partnership fit

GFS should prioritize partners whose values, audience, and presence strengthen the institution.

3. Shape the opportunity

Partnership opportunities may be built around programming, hospitality, education, festival presence, student access, or institutional support.
