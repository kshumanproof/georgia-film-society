Society Membership is for film lovers, writers, filmmakers, students, community members, and supporters who want member updates, access opportunities, eligible discounts, and a meaningful connection to Georgia Film Society’s year-round cultural work.

Featured benefits

Early Access

Early programming announcements and member access to select ticket sales.

Member Updates

Society news, member communications, and updates on programs, events, and institutional activity.

Recognition

Recognition as a Society Member, with website listing unless opted out.

Eligible Discounts

Up to 10% off eligible GFS programs, events, workshops, retreats, labs, and select affiliated opportunities.
