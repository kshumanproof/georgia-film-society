Volunteer

Help shape the atmosphere around cinema.

Georgia Film Society will rely on thoughtful, reliable volunteers to support screenings, salons, member gatherings, hospitality, student access, Cardennes-related programs, and future cultural events.

Volunteer Interest Student Access

Volunteer philosophy

Volunteers are not filler. They help create the tone, care, welcome, and operational seriousness people feel when they encounter the institution.

Volunteer areas

Support the experiences that make cinema culture feel alive.

01

Event Support

Help with guest flow, check-in, seating, wayfinding, event readiness, and the details that keep gatherings calm and polished.

02

Hospitality

Support filmmaker, member, guest, patron, and partner experiences with care, discretion, warmth, and professionalism.

03

Cardennes Support

Future festival support may include guest services, filmmaker hospitality, venue support, line management, and festival operations.

04

Student Volunteers

Students may support GFS while gaining exposure to event operations, cultural programming, film community, and institutional development.

05

Community Ambassadors

Help introduce aligned audiences, local communities, educators, civic contacts, and cultural supporters to GFS.

06

Operations

Support behind-the-scenes needs such as preparation, materials, guest lists, communications, and event follow-up.

Who we are looking for

Reliable, thoughtful people who understand the value of atmosphere.

Calm under pressure

Events move quickly. Volunteers should help make the room feel easier, not louder.

Guest-minded

GFS values hospitality, clear communication, and respect for members, artists, partners, and audiences.

Dependable

Showing up, following instructions, and communicating clearly matter. Glamour, tragically, is not the job description.

Aligned

Volunteers should understand that GFS is building a serious cultural institution, not casual event clutter.

Volunteer process

A simple intake now. A more formal system later.

1. Submit interest

Share your contact information, availability, location, relevant experience, and areas of interest.

2. Review and fit

GFS will review volunteer interest for alignment, timing, role fit, reliability, and event needs.

3. Orientation

As programming develops, selected volunteers may receive role expectations, event details, communication instructions, and basic orientation.
