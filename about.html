About Georgia Film Society

A cultural institution for cinema in Georgia and the Southeast.

Georgia Film Society exists to support the people who make, study, preserve, champion, and gather around film.

Founding belief

Cinema carries memory, culture, and consequence.

They always have. Long before film, television, or modern entertainment became an industry, people gathered around stories to preserve culture, share wisdom, confront fear, pass down memory, and better understand one another.

Cinema remains one of the most powerful forms of storytelling ever created. In an era of disposable content, algorithmic attention, and fragmented audiences, Georgia Film Society exists to make room for work that asks to be taken seriously.

Mission

Sustaining cultural infrastructure around film, artists, and audiences.

Georgia Film Society is a nonprofit cinematic cultural institution dedicated to artistic excellence, filmmaker support, and the enduring role of film in Georgia and the Southeast.

The Society is filmmaker-first and merit-first: it believes discovery, taste, and curation still matter. GFS would rather support work defined by craft and originality than chase trends.

Why Georgia

Production infrastructure alone does not create cultural leadership.

Georgia has become one of the most important production centers in modern entertainment. The state supports a growing ecosystem of filmmakers, writers, producers, craftspeople, entrepreneurs, investors, technologists, and creative leaders.

But great artistic regions are not built solely through soundstages, incentives, and studio spending. They are strengthened by institutions, artistic communities, hospitality, creative ambition, and places where the work is treated with care.

Georgia Film Society exists to help build what production alone cannot: a durable civic and artistic home for film.

Institutional values

Prestige over scale. Taste over trend. Institution over event.

01

Taste

GFS is guided by discernment, curation, and the belief that selection should mean something.

Filmmakers, members, patrons, students, partners, and guests should be treated with seriousness and care.

GFS is oriented toward permanence, not hype cycles, vanity metrics, or short-term attention.

Founder-led, institution-first

Georgia Film Society is founder-led, but built to stand as an institution: disciplined, enduring, and larger than any one person, event, or season.

Its purpose is to earn trust across Georgia’s film community and help create a lasting home for cinema, artists, audiences, and the people who support them.
