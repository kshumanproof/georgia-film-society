Frequently asked questions

A few practical answers about the institution.

What is Georgia Film Society? +

Georgia Film Society is a nonprofit cinematic cultural institution for Georgia and the Southeast, built around storytelling, cinema culture, filmmaker support, membership, patronage, partnerships, and year-round programming. Learn more on the About page.

Is GFS a nonprofit? +

Georgia Film Society is organized as a nonprofit cultural institution. Its public programming, membership, donor support, and partnerships are built with long-term cultural benefit in mind.

What is The Aster Film Festival? +

The Aster Film Festival is the flagship festival of Georgia Film Society. It is designed as a selective discovery platform shaped by merit, curation, hospitality, and serious cinematic work. Learn more on the The Aster page.

Is The Aster the same as GFS? +

No. Georgia Film Society is the larger institution. The Aster is the flagship festival brand inside the GFS ecosystem. The The Aster page explains that relationship.

How do I become a member? +

Visit the Membership page to choose an annual membership tier. Membership supports the institution while creating access to screenings, conversations, member events, early announcements, and cultural programming.

How do I donate? +

Visit the Support page to make a contribution or explore specific giving areas such as student access, programming, founding support, and institutional development.

Can my company sponsor or partner with GFS? +

Yes. GFS is building partnership pathways for sponsors, civic partners, hospitality partners, universities, technology companies, cultural organizations, and aligned institutions. Visit the Partner page to begin.

Where can I see current programs? +

Visit the Programs page for screenings, salons, education initiatives, student access, member events, The Aster-related updates, and the current institutional roadmap.

How can students or volunteers get involved? +

Students can visit the Student Access page. Volunteers can learn more on the Volunteer page or complete the volunteer application.

Who should press contact? +

Press can visit the Press page for approved language, institutional information, and media materials.
