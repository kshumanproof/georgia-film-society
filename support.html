Patronage philosophy

Supporters strengthen the institution that makes the work possible.

Supporters do more than fund individual events. They help create the cultural infrastructure that filmmakers, students, audiences, patrons, and partners can gather around for years to come.

Georgia Film Society is being built for longevity: thoughtful programming, filmmaker respect, student access, institutional hospitality, writer development, and a serious cultural home for cinema in Georgia and the Southeast.

Membership

Looking for annual membership instead of a donation?

Membership is the doorway into Georgia Film Society, with annual member tiers for Society, Inner Circle, and Patron Members. Students may request reduced Student Access Membership through the student pathway.
