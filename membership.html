Membership

Belong to Georgia’s growing cinema culture.

Membership is the doorway into Georgia Film Society — a direct way to help sustain the institution, with access to screenings, member events, and early announcements that aren't open to the public.

Membership philosophy

Membership is not a discount program. It is a way to participate in the formation of a cinematic cultural institution.

Annual membership

Choose your place in the institution.

Every membership supports Georgia Film Society’s year-round cultural work. Higher tiers add deeper access, stronger recognition, and closer participation.

Founding Circle

For patrons, families, executives, and institutional supporters shaping the Society’s long-term foundation.

Founding Circle is a relationship-based patron circle for major support, recognition, private conversations, and underwriting opportunities. It is not a standard checkout product.
