The Aster Film Festival

A prestige festival for serious cinematic discovery.

The Aster is the flagship festival of Georgia Film Society, centered on merit, curation, hospitality, and meaningful discovery.

Visit The Aster Join Updates

Festival positioning

The Aster is a boutique prestige festival where selection feels earned, hospitality shapes the experience, and serious cinematic work is treated with care.

Festival atmosphere

Cinema, hospitality, discovery, and serious creative work.

Curation

Selection should mean something.

The Aster is designed to feel selective, intentional, and filmmaker-respecting. The goal is not volume. The goal is discovery with credibility.

Filmmakers

Filmmaker-first, story-first, merit-first.

The Aster treats selected artists as serious professionals and gives the work a setting worthy of the craft behind it.

Relationship to GFS

The Aster Film Festival is the flagship. Georgia Film Society is the larger cultural engine.

The Festival

The Aster carries the festival experience.

Submissions, tickets, passes, schedules, festival press, sponsor details, and festival updates belong with The Aster.

The Institution

GFS builds the year-round culture around it.

Georgia Film Society remains the broader home for cinema culture, membership, patronage, education, partnerships, and long-term cultural infrastructure.

Hospitality

The experience begins before the first screening.

The Aster Film Festival is shaped by more than what appears on screen. Arrival, venue, conversation, hospitality, and a sense of welcome all contribute to the meaning of the festival.

The goal is a festival that feels refined without becoming distant, intimate without becoming small, and ambitious without becoming loud.

Host ecosystem

Designed for a destination-worthy festival experience.

The Aster is developed around walkability, hospitality, atmosphere, and a setting that supports filmmakers, patrons, guests, and serious cinematic conversation.

The festival should feel refined, accessible, filmmaker-centered, and connected to the larger cultural life surrounding it.

Festival bridge
