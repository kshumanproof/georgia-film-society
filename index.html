Story. Culture. Impact.

A cinematic cultural institution for Georgia and the future of story.

Georgia Film Society is building a year-round home for cinema, filmmakers, patrons, students, and audiences across Georgia and the Southeast.

Stay Connected Support the Society

Georgia Film Society exists to elevate cinema as culture. Through curated events, filmmaker-centered programming, membership experiences, educational initiatives, and future-facing conversations, GFS is building an institution designed to serve storytellers and audiences long after any single festival weekend ends.
