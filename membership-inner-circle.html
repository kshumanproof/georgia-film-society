Inner Circle Member is designed for supporters who want a closer connection to Georgia Film Society’s developing programming.

Join Inner Circle

What your membership supports

Your Inner Circle membership supports Georgia Film Society’s long-term mission while providing closer access to the Society’s developing programming calendar, member communications, screenings, filmmaker conversations, workshops, retreats, and future cultural events.

Featured benefits

Priority Updates

Priority member communications, priority programming updates, and priority event reminders.

Deeper Access

Invitations to future member screenings, salons, and filmmaker conversations as available.

Eligible Discounts

Up to 15% off eligible GFS programs, events, workshops, retreats, labs, and future affiliated submission opportunities.

Select Ticket Benefit

One complimentary ticket to a select future GFS screening or community event.
