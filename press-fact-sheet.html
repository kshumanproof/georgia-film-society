Institutional summary

Georgia Film Society is a nonprofit cinematic cultural institution for Georgia and the Southeast, dedicated to artistic excellence, filmmaker support, education, membership, patronage, partnerships, year-round programming, and The Aster Film Festival as its flagship festival.

Quick facts

Core public information.

Organization

Georgia Film Society

A nonprofit cinematic cultural institution serving Georgia and the Southeast.

Focus

Cinema culture

GFS supports film, filmmakers, audiences, students, patrons, partners, and year-round cultural programming.

Region

Georgia & the Southeast

The Society is rooted in Georgia while serving the broader creative and cinematic culture of the Southeast.

Flagship festival

The Aster Film Festival

The Aster is the flagship festival of Georgia Film Society.

Members, patrons, partners

Approved descriptions

Use these descriptions when referencing GFS.

Short description

Georgia Film Society is a nonprofit cinematic cultural institution for Georgia and the Southeast.

Standard description

Georgia Film Society is a nonprofit cinematic cultural institution dedicated to artistic excellence, filmmaker support, education, membership, patronage, partnerships, and the enduring role of film in Georgia and the Southeast.

The Aster

The Aster Film Festival is the flagship festival of Georgia Film Society, designed as a selective film festival shaped by merit, curation, hospitality, discovery, and cinematic distinction.

Usage note

Use institution-first language.

Use

Nonprofit cinematic cultural institution. Year-round cinema culture. Filmmaker-centered programming. The Aster Film Festival as the flagship festival of Georgia Film Society.

Avoid

Film club, casual meetup, festival-only organization, startup nonprofit, or generic arts organization.
