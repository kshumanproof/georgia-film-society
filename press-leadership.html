Governance

Officers provide leadership. The board supports oversight.

Georgia Film Society is guided by officers and board leadership responsible for mission alignment, institutional stewardship, public trust, and long-term cultural purpose.

President

Provides executive leadership, institutional direction, public representation, and strategic development.

Secretary

Supports governance records, board documentation, meeting minutes, and organizational continuity.

Treasurer

Supports financial oversight, reporting, budgeting, and responsible stewardship of institutional resources.

Board of Directors

Supports governance, mission integrity, strategic accountability, public trust, and long-term institutional credibility.
