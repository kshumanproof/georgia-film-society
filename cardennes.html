Crown jewel, not the whole crown

Cardennes is the flagship festival brand of Georgia Film Society, but GFS is the larger cultural engine.

The festival is designed to signal taste, discovery, hospitality, and artistic seriousness while remaining connected to the year-round institution around it.

Festival-specific programming, submissions, tickets, passes, schedules, sponsor information, press, and festival news should live at Cardennes.org.

Built for prestige, discovery, and hospitality.

Cardennes is intended to feel intimate, curated, sophisticated, and future-facing from the beginning.
