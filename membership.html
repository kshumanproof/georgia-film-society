Membership

Belong to the cultural life being built around cinema.

Membership is the doorway into Georgia Film Society. Members help sustain the institution while receiving access to screenings, conversations, member events, early announcements, and the year-round cinema culture being built across Georgia and the Southeast.

Membership philosophy

Membership is not a discount program. It is a way to participate in the formation of a cinematic cultural institution.
